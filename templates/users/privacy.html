Privacy Policy

📅 Last updated: 2 October 2026

Talanta Soka connects football players with verified scouts through structured football opportunities. Read our story →

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. It is written to meet the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. For any privacy question or request, contact us at {{ support_email }}.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: full name, email address, password (stored only in encrypted, hashed form), and whether you are a player or scout.
  • Player profiles: whether you play with the Stars (men’s football) or the Starlets (women’s football), used only to match you with the right scouts and trials; date of birth and age, playing position, county, height, weight, clubs, achievements, bio, profile photo, and the videos you upload.
  • Scout profiles: organization, specialization, profile photo, and the verification document you submit.
  • Optional contact details: an email or phone number you choose to share with scouts. A contact email is shown to scouts only after you confirm it with a code we email to it.
  • Activity: opportunities posted, applications made, scout feedback, replies, and the players a scout marks as interested.
  • Technical data: login sessions and verification codes needed to keep your account secure.

2. Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account, including sending verification and password-reset codes by email.
  • To show player profiles and videos to verified scouts, so players can be discovered.
  • To let scouts post trials and tournaments, and players apply to them.
  • To verify that scouts are genuine before they can post opportunities.
  • To produce reports that help administrators run and improve the platform.
  • To tell you about new features by email when something new arrives. Every update email has an Unsubscribe link; verification codes and important account emails still reach you.

We rely on your consent to process this information. You can withdraw it at any time by asking us to delete your account.

3. Players under 18

Players can join from age 12. A player under 18 must confirm that a parent or guardian has read this policy and the Terms of Use, and give us the parent or guardian's name and email address. We then email the parent or guardian a link to approve or decline the account. Until they approve, the player's profile and videos are hidden from all scouts and the player cannot apply for trials. We record when consent and approval were given. A parent or guardian can withdraw approval at any time using the same link, and can contact us to see, correct, or delete a child's information. We use the parent or guardian's email only for this approval.

4. Who can see your information

  • Verified scouts can see player profiles and videos. Your email or phone number is only shown to scouts if you choose to share it.
  • Administrators can see account details and scout verification documents, to manage and protect the platform.
  • Scout verification documents are never public. Only administrators review them.
  • The public (visitors who are not logged in) can only see the trials and tournaments page.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

5. Service providers and where data is stored

We use these providers to run the platform. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Render: hosts the website (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Neon: stores the database (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Cloudflare R2: stores photos, videos, and documents privately (Western Europe).
  • Brevo (France) and Google (Gmail): deliver verification, password-reset, and parent-approval emails.
  • Anthropic (United States): only if you press a “Write with AI” button, the rough notes you type there are sent to Anthropic’s Claude to write a draft for you. Nothing else about you is sent, the draft is not saved unless you save it, and Anthropic does not use these notes to train its AI. Please do not type phone numbers or other private details into the notes.

This means your information is stored outside Kenya, in the European Union, which has data protection laws at least as strong as Kenya's. Files are kept in private storage and are only viewable through short-lived links shown to logged-in users.

6. Cookies and what we save on your device

Cookies are small notes a website saves in your browser so it remembers you as you move between pages. Talanta Soka uses only the cookies it needs to work. We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, and no other company places cookies through our site.

  • sessionid: keeps you logged in. It is removed when you log out and otherwise expires after two weeks. Page scripts cannot read it.
  • csrftoken: a security check that stops other websites from sending forms while pretending to be you. It lasts up to one year.

Both cookies are sent only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Because they are essential for logging in and for security, they do not need your permission, but you can delete them at any time in your browser settings; you will then need to log in again.

We also remember a few choices only on your own device (not sent to us): light or dark mode, English or Swahili, whether you have seen the welcome tour or the latest “What’s new” update, and when to ask “How is Talanta Soka?”. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Our landing page loads its lettering from Google Fonts, so Google receives your device’s internet address when that page opens; it does not set cookies. If we ever add analytics or advertising, we will ask for your permission first and update this policy.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When an account is deleted, we delete its profile, uploaded photos, videos, and documents. Verification codes expire after 15 minutes.

8. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used;
  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong, most of which you can edit yourself on your profile;
  • have your information deleted;
  • object to or withdraw consent for its use.

To use any of these rights, email {{ support_email }}.

If you are not satisfied, feel unheard, or have been mistreated or abused in any way, kindly talk to us. We will listen, take it seriously, and do our best to make it right. Contact us at {{ support_email }}.

If you are still not satisfied after talking to us, you can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) of Kenya.

9. Security

Passwords are hashed, the site only runs over encrypted HTTPS connections, accounts are verified by email, and uploaded files are stored privately. After several wrong passwords or codes, further attempts are paused for 15 minutes, and we email you if your password or login email changes. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and keep it to yourself.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and let registered users know.
