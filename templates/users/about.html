Our story

It starts with a child.

Shoes worn thin, knees covered in dust, walking home late in the evening after hours of kucheza kwa vumbi 😅 on the estate pitch. Supper goes cold while their eyes stay glued to the TV: Messi gliding past three defenders, Ronaldo rising high for a header. And quietly, a dream is born: one day, that could be me.

“Nionekane nikicheza kwa TV!”

Across Kenya, from estate pitches to village fields, thousands of children carry that same dream. Many have magic in their feet. But too often nobody who matters is watching: no scout was there that day, and the talent fades away unseen.

Talanta Soka was born to change that. It started as an academic project at Strathmore University, built by a team of ambitious, driven BBIT students who grew up loving the game and believe one simple thing: your talent deserves to be seen.

We are here to put your skills in front of the right people, open doors to real trials and opportunities, and walk with you every step of the way. Your dream is our mission, and we want the very best for you.

We believe every talent is a gift from God, given to be used and shared. No player grows alone: teammates push you, coaches correct you, scouts open doors, and you lift the next child coming up behind you. That is the spirit of Talanta Soka.

“As iron sharpens iron, so one person sharpens another.”

Why we exist

Too many gifted players never get a fair chance, and scouts struggle to find them. Talanta Soka brings both together in one safe place: players show their game, verified scouts discover them, and real trials and tournaments are open to everyone. No connections needed, just your game.

How it works

⚽ For players

  1. Sign up free as a Star or a Starlet.
  2. Build your profile and upload your best videos.
  3. Apply to trials and get feedback from scouts.

🔭 For scouts

  1. Sign up and send a document to be verified.
  2. Discover players by position, age and county.
  3. Post trials and tournaments in minutes.

Kuhusu sisi

Hadithi yetu

Inaanza na mtoto.

Viatu vimechakaa, magoti yamejaa vumbi, anarudi nyumbani jioni sana baada ya masaa ya kucheza kwa vumbi 😅 kwenye uwanja wa mtaa. Chakula kinapoa huku macho yake yamekodolewa kwenye TV: Messi akiwapita mabeki watatu, Ronaldo akiruka juu kupiga kichwa. Na kimya kimya, ndoto inazaliwa: siku moja, hiyo inaweza kuwa mimi.

“Nionekane nikicheza kwa TV!”

Kote Kenya, kutoka viwanja vya mtaa hadi vya vijijini, maelfu ya watoto wanabeba ndoto hiyo hiyo. Wengi wana vipaji vya ajabu miguuni. Lakini mara nyingi hakuna mtu muhimu anayeangalia: hakuna scout aliyekuwepo siku hiyo, na talanta inapotea bila kuonekana.

Talanta Soka ilizaliwa kubadilisha hilo. Ilianza kama mradi wa masomo katika Chuo Kikuu cha Strathmore, ikijengwa na timu ya wanafunzi wa BBIT wenye ndoto kubwa na bidii waliokua wakipenda mpira, na wanaoamini jambo moja: talanta yako inastahili kuonekana.

Tuko hapa kuweka ujuzi wako mbele ya watu sahihi, kufungua milango ya trials na fursa halisi, na kutembea nawe kila hatua. Ndoto yako ndiyo dhamira yetu, na tunakutakia kilicho bora zaidi.

Tunaamini kila talanta ni zawadi kutoka kwa Mungu, iliyotolewa ili itumike na kushirikiwa. Hakuna mchezaji anayekua peke yake: wachezaji wenzako wanakusukuma, makocha wanakurekebisha, ma-scout wanafungua milango, nawe unamwinua mtoto anayekuja nyuma yako. Hiyo ndiyo roho ya Talanta Soka.

“Chuma hunoa chuma; ndivyo mtu amnoavyo rafiki yake.”

Kwa nini tupo

Wachezaji wengi wenye vipaji hawapati nafasi ya haki, na ma-scout wanapata shida kuwapata. Talanta Soka inawaleta pamoja mahali salama: wachezaji wanaonyesha game yao, ma-scout walio-verified wanawagundua, na trials na mashindano ya kweli yako wazi kwa wote. Huhitaji connection, ni game yako tu.

Inavyofanya kazi

⚽ Kwa wachezaji

  1. Jisajili bure kama Star au Starlet.
  2. Tengeneza profile na upakie video zako bora.
  3. Omba trials na upate maoni kutoka kwa ma-scout.

🔭 Kwa ma-scout

  1. Jisajili na utume hati ya uthibitisho.
  2. Gundua wachezaji kwa nafasi, umri na kaunti.
  3. Weka trials na mashindano kwa dakika chache.
