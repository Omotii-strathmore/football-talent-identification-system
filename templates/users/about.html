About us

Who we are

Talanta Soka was born as an academic project at Strathmore University, built by a team of ambitious, driven BBIT students who love football and believe in one simple idea: your talent deserves to be seen. Across Kenya, from estate pitches to village fields, there are players with magic in their feet who are never noticed, just because no scout was there that day. That is the dream we are chasing with you: to see you on the big stage, and to hear you say “Nionekane nikicheza kwa TV!”

Why we exist

Too many gifted players never get a fair chance, and scouts struggle to find them. Talanta Soka brings both together in one safe place: players show their game, verified scouts discover them, and real trials and tournaments are open to everyone. No connections needed, just your game.

How it works

⚽ For players

  1. Sign up free as a Star or a Starlet.
  2. Build your profile and upload your best videos.
  3. Apply to trials and get feedback from scouts.

🔭 For scouts

  1. Sign up and send a document to be verified.
  2. Discover players by position, age and county.
  3. Post trials and tournaments in minutes.

Our promises to you

  • ✅ Only verified scouts can see players. Our team checks every scout’s documents first.
  • 👪 Players under 18 stay hidden until a parent or guardian approves.
  • 🚫 Scouts never ask for money to scout, to attend a trial, or to join a club.
  • 🔒 Your data stays private, and you decide what to share. Read our Privacy Policy.
  • 🚩 Something wrong? Report a concern anytime. We read every report.

Kuhusu sisi

Talanta yako inastahili kuonekana.

“Nionekane nikicheza kwa TV!”

Sisi ni nani

Talanta Soka ilianza kama mradi wa masomo katika Chuo Kikuu cha Strathmore, ikijengwa na timu ya wanafunzi wa BBIT wenye ndoto kubwa na bidii wanaopenda mpira na wanaoamini jambo moja: talanta yako inastahili kuonekana. Kote Kenya, kutoka viwanja vya mtaa hadi vya kijijini, kuna wachezaji wenye vipaji ambao hawaonekani kwa sababu tu hakuna scout aliyekuwepo siku hiyo. Hiyo ndiyo ndoto tunayoifuata pamoja nawe: kukuona kwenye jukwaa kubwa, na kukusikia ukisema “Nionekane nikicheza kwa TV!”

Kwa nini tupo

Wachezaji wengi wenye vipaji hawapati nafasi ya haki, na ma-scout wanapata shida kuwapata. Talanta Soka inawaleta pamoja mahali salama: wachezaji wanaonyesha game yao, ma-scout walio-verified wanawagundua, na trials na mashindano ya kweli yako wazi kwa wote. Huhitaji connection, ni game yako tu.

Inavyofanya kazi

⚽ Kwa wachezaji

  1. Jisajili bure kama Star au Starlet.
  2. Tengeneza profile na upakie video zako bora.
  3. Omba trials na upate maoni kutoka kwa ma-scout.

🔭 Kwa ma-scout

  1. Jisajili na utume hati ya uthibitisho.
  2. Gundua wachezaji kwa nafasi, umri na kaunti.
  3. Weka trials na mashindano kwa dakika chache.
