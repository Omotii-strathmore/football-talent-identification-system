Terms of Use

📅 Last updated: 30 September 2026

These terms are the rules for using Talanta Soka. By creating an account, you agree to them and to our Privacy Policy.

1. Who can join

  • Players must be between 12 and 28 years old. Talanta Soka welcomes both men’s football (Stars) and women’s football (Starlets); each player chooses their category so we can match them with the right scouts and trials.
  • Players under 18 need a parent or guardian to agree. We email the parent or guardian to approve the account, and scouts cannot see the player until they do.
  • Scouts must represent a genuine club, academy, or football organization and submit a valid verification document. Scouts can only view player profiles and post opportunities after an administrator approves them.

2. Your account

  • Give true and accurate information, including your real name and date of birth.
  • Keep your password private. You are responsible for activity on your account.
  • One person, one account. Do not create accounts for other people without their permission.

3. Videos and content you upload

  • Only upload videos and photos that you have the right to share, and that show you playing football.
  • No violent, sexual, hateful, or abusive content, and no content that shows other children without their guardian's permission.
  • You keep ownership of what you upload. You allow Talanta Soka to store it and show it to logged-in users of the platform, only for talent identification. This stops when you delete the content or your account.

4. Rules for scouts

  • Only post real trials and tournaments, with accurate details and deadlines.
  • Never ask a player or their family for money to be scouted, to attend a trial, or to join a club.
  • Contact players under 18 only through the platform or in the presence of their parent or guardian.
  • Use player information only for scouting, and never share it outside the platform.
  • Treat every player with respect, Stars and Starlets alike. Harassment, sexual comments, threats, or pressure of any kind are never allowed and lead to immediate removal from Talanta Soka, and may be reported to the authorities.

5. Safety and reporting

If anyone asks you for money, makes you feel unsafe, harasses you, or behaves inappropriately, stop contacting them and use the Report a concern link on the site, or report it to {{ support_email }}. For anything involving a risk to a child, also contact the police or Childline Kenya on 116.

6. What we can and cannot promise

  • Talanta Soka helps players get seen. We do not guarantee trials, selection, contracts, or payment.
  • We verify scouts' documents, but we cannot guarantee every scout's intentions. Always use care, especially when meeting in person.
  • The platform may sometimes be slow or unavailable, especially while it runs on free hosting.

7. Suspending or removing accounts

We may suspend or delete accounts that break these terms, post false information, or put other users at risk. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

8. Changes and governing law

We may update these terms and will change the date at the top when we do. These terms are governed by the laws of Kenya.
